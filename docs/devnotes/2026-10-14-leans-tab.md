## Why

Round 1 of "Matchups & betting" picked a dedicated tab (A2) named **Leans** — not "Bets", so nobody thinks
Pare places bets — with the G2 game screen, player vs defense (B1) and user-typed prop lines (B2). Goal: put
the line next to the data that explains it, and say so only when the data clearly agrees (3 signals).

## Spread / moneyline rule (proposed here, per DECISIONS §6)

Three team-data reads, all for the same side S: last-4 average point margin ≥ 8 better · season point
differential rank ≥ 10 places better · S would have covered today's line in ≥ 3 of its last 4 while the other
side covered in ≤ 1 (moneyline: S won ≥ 3 of 4, the other ≤ 1). Deliberately rare. Numbers in `config.ts`.

## Open risks

- ESPN odds are unofficial; a missing `moneyline` shows "—" and "No lean".
- `/api/leans/data` isn't gzipped (~65 KB for two teams); fine on phones today, gzip like `/api/live/*` if it grows.
- Home's week refresh / live merge compare spread + O/U only, so a moneyline-only move appears with the next
  spread/total change or a reload (Home logic deliberately not touched).
- Cold Sleeper player map (~20 MB) can slow the first Leans request after a deploy (Leaders usually warms it).
- The inline Compare panels on the game screen ignore team-change taps (no-op handlers) — the hero names
  aren't tappable there by design; the panel's own pickers do nothing.
- Licensing: same as the rest of the app (ESPN unofficial, Sleeper non-commercial) — licensed odds/data needed
  before any Pro tier that sells leans.
