Shows: your "drop down from the teams" idea. Under the team names sits one button: Compare these teams ▾. Closed, the screen is just lines + props.

NOWfree

Why a button, not tapping the names: on the Compare tab, tapping a team name already means "change team". If the same tap meant "show stats" here, the app would teach two meanings for one gesture. A button right under the names keeps the idea without the clash.
