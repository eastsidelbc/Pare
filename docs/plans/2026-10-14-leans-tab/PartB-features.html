Recommendation

Placement: A2 (Leans tab) with the G2 game screen — the one you picked, and I agree. Betting info gets one clear home that you open on purpose, so Home stays a fast schedule and Compare stays a pure stats tool. G2 keeps the team stats one tap away on every bet screen without making it 3 screens long, and it's the same Compare component, so there's nothing new to maintain. The cost is that Standings leaves the nav for a Home header button — mid-season that's the right trade, and you can revisit it in December when playoff races matter.

Worth paying for: B3 Edge vs line and B4 Biggest edges (they need a paid odds feed + a backtested projection model, so they should earn their keep), B5 AI summary (costs money per use), and B7 saved views (cheap to build, easy to understand as a perk). Keep free: B1, B2 (the core of Pare — people need to trust the leans before they'll pay), B8, and basic B6.
